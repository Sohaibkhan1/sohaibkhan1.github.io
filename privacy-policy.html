# Privacy Policy – BlueHole **Last Updated: August 20, 2026** BlueHole is designed to provide a simple and privacy-conscious way to download publicly available video content. This Privacy Policy explains what information and permissions may be used by the application and why. ## 1. Information We Collect BlueHole does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, password, contacts, or other personally identifiable information. BlueHole does not intentionally store your personal videos, photos, audio files, or downloaded media on our servers. Your downloaded content remains on your device. ## 2. Storage and Media Permissions BlueHole may request access to device storage or media files because the application needs to save downloaded videos and audio files to your device. These permissions may also be required to display, manage, share, or otherwise provide features related to media stored on your device. Storage or media access is used for application functionality and is not intended to collect your personal files for unrelated purposes. BlueHole does not intentionally upload your personal photos, videos, or audio files to our servers. ## 3. Location Permission BlueHole may request location permission for application analytics and administration purposes. When location permission is granted, the application may use location information to determine approximate geographic information, such as the user's country or general location, for the application's internal dashboard and usage monitoring system. This functionality is connected with our application management and analytics dashboard powered by Supabase. Location information is used to help us understand general application usage, monitor application activity, and improve the application's operation and reliability. Location information is not intended to be used to identify individual users personally. If you do not grant location permission, the main downloading functionality of BlueHole can still be used, although certain analytics or application-management features may not function as intended. ## 4. Supabase and Application Dashboard BlueHole uses Supabase as part of its application management and analytics infrastructure. The application may send limited technical information to our Supabase-powered dashboard for purposes such as: • Monitoring application activity • Understanding general usage statistics • Monitoring active devices • Identifying general geographic information such as country • Maintaining application performance and reliability • Detecting technical issues and improving the application BlueHole does not intentionally use this system to store users' downloaded videos, photos, audio files, passwords, contacts, or other personal media. ## 5. Device Information For application functionality, security, analytics, and usage monitoring, BlueHole may process limited technical information associated with a device. This may include information such as a device identifier, application activity status, timestamps, and general geographic information when the relevant permission is granted. Such information is used for legitimate application-management and operational purposes and is not intended to personally identify the user. ## 6. Downloaded Content BlueHole is intended to download content that is publicly available and legally permitted to be downloaded. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary permission or legal rights to download, save, use, reproduce, or share any content. BlueHole does not claim ownership of downloaded third-party content. Users should respect copyright, intellectual property rights, privacy rights, and the terms of the original content provider. ## 7. Personal Media BlueHole does not intentionally upload or store your personal media files on its servers. Videos, images, and audio files downloaded or managed through the application remain on your device unless you choose to share or otherwise transfer them using Android or another service. ## 8. Third-Party Services BlueHole may use third-party services that are necessary for application functionality, analytics, advertising, infrastructure, or other technical purposes. Currently, Supabase may be used for the application's backend analytics and management dashboard. Third-party service providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. BlueHole does not sell your personal information. ## 9. Advertising If advertisements are displayed within BlueHole, advertising providers may independently process certain technical information as permitted by their services and applicable laws. BlueHole does not sell personal information to advertisers. Users may be presented with advertising based on the configuration of the application and applicable advertising-provider policies. ## 10. Data Retention BlueHole does not intentionally retain users' personal media files on its servers. Limited technical or analytics information used by the application's backend may be retained only as necessary for application administration, monitoring, security, troubleshooting, and improvement. ## 11. Data Sharing BlueHole does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not intentionally provide users' personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate application infrastructure, analytics, advertising, security, or other technical functionality. ## 12. Data Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with the application. Data transmitted between the application and supported backend services may be protected using appropriate security mechanisms, such as encrypted network communication where supported. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 13. Children's Privacy BlueHole does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the application inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter. ## 14. Permissions Depending on the Android version and features being used, BlueHole may request permissions including: **Storage / Photos and Videos / Media Access:** Used to save downloaded media and provide media-related functionality. **Location:** Used for application analytics, general geographic information, and the application's Supabase-powered management dashboard. Permissions can generally be managed through your device's Android settings. ## 15. Your Choices You can deny optional permissions through your device settings. You can also stop using the application or uninstall it at any time. Please note that disabling certain permissions may prevent related features from working correctly. ## 16. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the application, backend services, permissions, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The latest version will be made available through the application or its official distribution page. ## 17.
